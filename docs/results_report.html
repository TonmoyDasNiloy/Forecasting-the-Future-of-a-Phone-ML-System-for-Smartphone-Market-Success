1. Experimental Setup

All models are evaluated with GroupShuffleSplit (80% train / 20% test) where the group key is the phone identity (brand + model). This guarantees a phone never appears in both folds, eliminating memorisation and giving an honest generalisation estimate. Cold-start models (Pillars 2 & 4) use only launch-known features; post-launch outcomes are never inputs. Random state = 42.
